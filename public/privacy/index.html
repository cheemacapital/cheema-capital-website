Cheema Capital ("we," "us," or "our") provides automated call handling and lead notification services for businesses.

Information We Collect

We may collect information provided through our services, including:

  • Names
  • Phone numbers
  • Business contact information
  • Service addresses
  • Information provided during phone calls
  • Call transcripts and summaries
  • Service request details
  • SMS messaging preferences and consent information

How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • Provide and operate our call handling and lead notification services
  • Deliver service-request information to participating businesses
  • Send operational SMS notifications to recipients who have consented to receive them
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve our services
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents

SMS Messaging

Recipients may opt in to receive automated SMS notifications related to inbound leads and service requests.

Message frequency varies based on inbound call and lead volume.

Message and data rates may apply.

Recipients may reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages and HELP for assistance.

Mobile Information and Consent

We do not share, sell, rent, or provide mobile phone numbers or SMS messaging consent information to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Service Providers

We may use service providers to operate our services, including communications, automation, hosting, and infrastructure providers. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide services on our behalf and subject to applicable contractual and privacy obligations.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information processed through our services.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Your Choices

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.

For assistance with SMS messages, reply HELP or contact us using the information below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
